About SafeTrace AI

Leveraging artificial intelligence to transform public safety and missing person recovery.

Our Mission

Bringing Missing People Home

SafeTrace AI is a capstone project developed at Manipal University Jaipur that integrates facial recognition technology with geo-tagging and real-time tracking to create a comprehensive missing person detection and safety system.

Our platform addresses the critical gap in existing missing person infrastructure by automating the identification process and enabling real-time collaboration between law enforcement agencies, NGOs, and families.

By leveraging state-of-the-art deep learning models, we reduce identification time from days to minutes, significantly increasing the chances of safe recovery.

Goals

Project Objectives

Our research-driven approach focuses on five core objectives.

Automatic Detection

Develop an automated system for detecting and identifying missing persons through advanced facial recognition algorithms.

Integrated Tracking

Integrate facial recognition with geo-tagging for comprehensive tracking and location-based search capabilities.

Interactive Dashboard

Design a user-friendly dashboard for families and authorities to monitor cases, view matches, and manage investigations.

Data Privacy

Ensure strict data privacy and security compliance through encryption and role-based access control mechanisms.

Real-Time Alerts

Implement a real-time notification system that immediately alerts stakeholders when potential matches are identified.

AI Accuracy

Achieve high facial recognition accuracy (>95%) across diverse demographics, lighting conditions, and image quality levels.
