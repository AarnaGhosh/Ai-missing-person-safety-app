FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about SafeTrace AI.

Our system uses deep learning-based facial recognition (similar to FaceNet and ArcFace architectures) to create a unique 128-dimensional embedding of each face. When a new photo is uploaded, the system compares this embedding against our database of known missing persons. The AI analyzes facial features including bone structure, eye spacing, and other unique markers, providing a confidence score for each potential match. This process happens in real-time and works even with varying lighting conditions, angles, and partial occlusions.
Absolutely. SafeTrace AI implements enterprise-grade security measures including AES-256 encryption for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for data in transit, and role-based access control (RBAC). We follow GDPR and Indian data protection guidelines. Only authorized personnel can access case data, and all access is logged with complete audit trails. Photos and personal information are never shared with third parties.
Anyone can file a missing person report through our platform -- family members, friends, neighbors, or authorities. We recommend also filing an official FIR (First Information Report) with your local police station. Our platform works alongside law enforcement, not as a replacement. For children under 18, reports are given priority processing.
Our facial recognition system achieves over 98% accuracy on standard benchmarks (LFW, CFP). In real-world conditions, accuracy varies based on image quality, lighting, and age difference between photos. Clear, front-facing photos with good lighting yield the best results. The system also performs age progression estimation for cases involving children who have been missing for extended periods.
After submission, the AI immediately begins analyzing the uploaded photos and searching for matches. You'll receive a case ID and can track progress through the dashboard. If matches are found, you and registered authorities will receive real-time notifications. The system continuously monitors new data and sightings, running periodic re-analyses to catch new matches as they become available.
Yes, SafeTrace AI is completely free for families and individuals reporting missing persons. The platform is developed as a public safety initiative by Manipal University Jaipur. Law enforcement agencies and NGOs can also use the platform at no cost. We are funded through university research grants and aim to keep the platform accessible to everyone.
