How It Works

Our streamlined 4-step process leverages AI to accelerate the search and recovery of missing persons.

1

Upload Photo

Submit photos and details of the missing person through our secure platform.

2

AI Analysis

Our facial recognition engine analyzes and matches against databases in real-time.

3

Geo-Tracking

Location data and sightings are mapped for coordinated search efforts.

4

Alert & Recover

Instant alerts are sent to authorities and families when matches are found.
